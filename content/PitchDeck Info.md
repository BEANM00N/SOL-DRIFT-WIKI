### **SLIDE 5: THE VISION**

SOL DRIFT’s vision is to deliver "Kinetic Exorcism"—the catharsis of overcoming overwhelming odds through pure skill and motion.

Our signature **DRIFT mechanic** reinvents flight combat, creating an aggressive dance of evasion and attack essential for surviving its relentless arenas. We blend the pure, satisfying combat loop of **_ULTRAKILL_** with the flight fantasy of **_Starfox_** and customisation culture of ***Need for Speed***, all set against a backdrop of mechanical existentialism, reminiscent of ***Armored Core***.

Become the ultimate predator in a haunted house - you are the thing the phantoms fear, purging them through a visceral and violent dance.

---

### **SLIDE 6: THE PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY**

The market shows a clear and proven appetite for high-skill, single-player action games that deliver a focused and satisfying core loop. However, the flight combat genre often dilutes this pure action with complex simulation or objectives that create lulls in gameplay -  an issue our own player testing and fans of the genre have consistently highlighted.

SOL DRIFT addresses this gap directly. By stripping away all extraneous elements, we deliver a highly focused, viscerally rewarding arcade experience centered on pure arena combat. Our pivot to this design is a direct response to observed player desire, de-risking the core premise and ensuring development is focused entirely on what keeps players engaged: a tight combat loop, challenging enemy variations, and the DUAL Movement system that holds it all together.

### **SLIDE 9: KEY FEATURES**

- **Signature DRIFT Mechanic:** A transformative flight model for aggressive, kinetic survival.

---

### **SLIDE 10: CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP**

- **Second-to-Second:** Intense, unbroken dogfighting. Using DRIFT for constant repositioning, evasion, and lining up shots against overwhelming numbers of enemies. Chaining kills and stylish manoeuvres to earn MERITS and stay alive.
- **Arena-to-Arena (Half-Hour-to-Half-Hour):**
    1. **Hangar & Build Customization:** (Blockout Phase) Players upgrade their ship and select their next combat arena.
    2. **Arena Encounter:** (Core Implemented) Survive escalating waves of enemies in a contained arena. The only objective is annihilation.
    3. **Progression:** (Early Development) Use rewards from combat to unlock new parts, weapons, and perks, enabling new builds to tackle tougher arenas and climb the leaderboard.

---

### **SLIDE 11: META / PROGRESSION SYSTEMS (Replayability)**

- **MERIT System:** A core progression vector encouraging players to master different skills and replay arenas to achieve specific combat accolades. (Similar to Gears of War Medals, however they actually reward the player).
- **Ship Upgrades & Perks:** (In early development) Rewards from combat will be used to unlock new parts, allowing players to create specialised builds (e.g., glass cannon, tank) tailored to their playstyle.
- **Public Leaderboards:** (Live on website) The ultimate driver for replayability, tracking pure combat performance scores and completion times for community bragging rights.
- **Arena Unlocks:** (Planned) Success in lower-tier arenas will unlock new, more dangerous pocket dimensions with unique environmental hazards and tougher phantom configurations.

### **SLIDE 15: BUSINESS MODEL**

- **Premium Title:** SOL DRIFT will be a premium, one-time purchase game.
- **Focus:** Delivering a complete and polished single-player arena combat experience.
- **No Microtransactions (MTX):** Our design philosophy is to protect the integrity of the skill-based gameplay. Any potential post-launch content would be substantial DLC (e.g., new arena packs, enemy types) and never compromise the core experience.
