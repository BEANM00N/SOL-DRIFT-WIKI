# **MAD MOON STUDIOS – PROJECT SNAPSHOT: SOL DRIFT**

_May 2025 Update_

---

### 🎮 **Project Summary**

**SOL DRIFT** reimagines the arcade space shooter with expressive, high-skill dogfighting built around our unique **DRIFT mechanic** - a zero-G, handbrake-like pivot that makes combat fluid, aggressive, and cinematic. Players face bizarre cult factions across fractured star systems, chained together by fast, stylish gameplay and narrative flare.
