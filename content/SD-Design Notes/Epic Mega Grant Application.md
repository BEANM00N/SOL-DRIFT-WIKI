### Full project details
##### Project Description and next steps

**SOL DRIFT** reimagines the arcade space shooter with a focus on frenetic and expressive dogfighting. At its core is our signature **DRIFT** mechanic that flips standard dogfighting combat on it's head. This is experienced through a gauntlet of challenging and varied encounters against the numerous cults of personalities and their followers that populate the lawless and outright bizarre regions of space that exist on the fringes of a fractured universe. SOL DRIFT has a highly focused approach on ensuring that player to game inputs are exciting and viscerally rewarding to execute from purely a cause and effect interaction stand point, so that even in a vacuum SOL DRIFT is fun to play! 

SOL DRIFT is developed by MAD MOON STUDIOS, a small team based in the city of Belfast, Northern Ireland. As a studio, "Creating fun in the chaotic" encapsulates our philosophy. Founded by a few ambitious game designers, our journey began with a shared vision to reignite the free flowing fun we remember from games that inspired us growing up. We take pride in our approach to making unforgettable gaming experiences by creating games we always wanted to play yet didn't exist.

The project has resonated strongly at industry events, including **QCon Belfast**, **RENDR Festival**, **Dublin Comic Con**, and a recent showcase at **BAFTA London** via the NI Screen Showcase, where we had the honour to show off our work alongside some of the most creative people in our local industry. ### Please tell us about some of the unique features of your project.

SOL DRIFT grants a lot of "what if's" when it comes to gameplay, "What if we stuck a rotating tank cannon on the back of a space jet?" or "What if we let the player shoot down enemy missiles with guns" and even "what if we give the player a means to repulse said missiles as a form of parrying back toward the enemy that fired them?"

**DRIFT:** SOL DRIFT's flight model revolves around the **DRIFT**, enabling pilots to instantly decouple their ship's pivot for aiming and executing tight turns, akin to a drift car's handbrake turn, except in zero-G. The result is a fluid, almost skate-like dance of evasion and attack, prioritizing agility and finesse over static firepower, distinguishing it heavily from traditional flight games while maintaining its intuitive aeronautics of traditional flying mechanics, all wrapped together by procedural animation powered by UE's Animation Blueprints, allowing us to quickly iterate and finetune game feel.

**MERIT System:** Beyond point scoring, SOL DRIFT features MERITS - in game medals awarded for achieving specific manoeuvres. Ranging from chaining kills while actively drifting, dodging incoming missiles with style, or using the environment against the enemy - Overall, MERITS provide the player with an ever-growing incentive to show off new skills accompanied by instant visual and auditory feedback, score bonuses, and is tracked via an active progression System.

**Public Leader boards:** Hosted live on our website (www.madmoonstudios.co.uk/leaderboard), the Leaderboards offer more than just overall high scores. They track performance across numerous statistics, each designed to test different facets of our player's skills and progression. Under the hood, this relies on UE's HTTPS system, which allows us to seamlessly integrate backend services like Google Firebase and other APIs for reliable, real-time data handling. From experience at various conventions, these Leaderboards significantly enhance the **social experience**, providing a friendly competitive avenue, encouraging repeat play, and boosting long-term community engagement.

**Accessible Fun, High Skill Ceiling:** Designed for Rapid Plug and Play fun, SOL DRIFT avoids immediate complexity. The core mechanics are intuitive, but the DRIFT system and nuances within the rest of the movement allow for immense depth. Experienced players can chain movements, manipulate momentum precisely, and perform intricate manoeuvres, creating a high skill ceiling that encourages mastery and delivers cinematic moments with significant replay value. This accessibility extends beyond gameplay to the hardware level. By leveraging UE's powerful optimization tools, including its various profilers and flexible shading options, we are focused on striking a great balance between performance, visual fidelity and most importantly, visual clarity! This ensures the game is accessible to a wider range of players, currently demonstrated by running smoothly at **at least 100fps at native 1080p on a desktop RTX 3060**.
